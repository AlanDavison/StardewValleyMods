# Invisible Buffs

All releases can be found on my [Nexus page](https://www.nexusmods.com/users/79440738?tab=user+files).

### What it does
Allows content pack authors to make their custom buffs not render in the HUD.

### Extra information
This assumes you're already familiar with basic Content Patcher, and have a custom buff (or are editing an existing buff) you want to make invisible in the HUD. 
You just need to add a CustomField named DH.Buffs.Invisible.

The value can be empty, or anything you like. Maybe leave a joke easter egg for people looking through your mod's files!
If you need help, or find a problem, ping me (@decidedlyhuman) in #making-mods-general in the Stardew Valley Discord server.
